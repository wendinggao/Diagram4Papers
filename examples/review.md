# Native render review

The current `autogps.png` is the diagrams.net native export at scale 2 of the adjacent editable file. The full canvas and its dense proof/engine regions were inspected. Text, geometry, dependency edges and formula records remain native draw.io objects; external illustrations are ledger-backed SVGs.

The earlier composition put too much of the scientific work into a large algebra strip and sparse proof rows. The revised right column exposes four atomic transformations with concrete expressions, gives known facts and theorem applications visible professional icons, adds target matching and bounded continuation, and replaces generic omitted nodes with valid unused geometric results. The final proof rows show premises as well as the operation and output. Icon footprints were enlarged after the first density revision so that more assets would not merely become small decorations.

The final image was checked for text/frame collisions, crowded icon-label pairs, formula separation, grouping visibility and consistent type. Larger objects and compact rows replaced unnecessary whitespace; the proof-selection footer was removed rather than retained as tiny text. Tool checks separately verify font advances, XML, frame tokens and exact artwork provenance. These checks do not certify aesthetics.

Scientific checks distinguish MPF syntax/alignment from DSR geometric validation, identify the contradiction as a rejected candidate, retain the positive-length condition, and distinguish illustrative grouped derivations from a captured solver trace. The geometry is locally checked, including an independent area calculation.

This is an author-reviewed public example. It has not undergone independent agent evaluation and does not establish that the Skill transfers successfully to every paper. Visual quality remains open to user review.
